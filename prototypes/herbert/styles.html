Herbert — three styles

Same colours, same frame (219 px in the 251 px panel). Pick one and the states get redrawn in that style.

A · Cartoon pony

Classic 3/4 view, standing, turned toward the page. The most "unicorn" silhouette; long muzzle, mane down the neck, four legs, satchel on the flank.

B · Plush, front view

Faces you like a soft toy, but with a horse's head: tall skull, muzzle at the bottom, eyes high and wide apart, tall ears, big horn, mane falling to the shoulders.

C · Flat mascot bust

Head and neck only, in profile, thick lines, flat colours: the Flash-era sticker look. Big eye, long muzzle, an envelope in his mouth. Simplest to animate, strongest at small size.
