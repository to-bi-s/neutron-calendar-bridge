Herbert the unicorn

A first look at the Pro-Version companion, on his own before he moves into the app. He breathes, blinks, follows the pointer, glances at whatever you click, and says a few words. Click anywhere on this page and watch him look.

State

What the app will set from the sync engine: the connection is gone, a sync failed, all is well.

Words

The greeting plays once when the window opens, by the hour. Encouragements come now and then on their own (every two minutes on this page) and only when nothing else is going on.

Reactions

Twenty things that happen while the bridge runs, each with a pose, a prop and a line. Hover a button for the event behind it. Clicking Herbert himself is a poke.

Appearance

As in the app: the panel stays dark purple, the page and Herbert's speech bubble follow the theme.

What the app will call

Also available in the browser console as herbert — try herbert.say('hello') or herbert.setState('asleep') or herbert.react('delivered').
