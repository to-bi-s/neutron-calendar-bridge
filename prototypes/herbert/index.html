Herbert the unicorn

A first look at the Pro-Version companion, on his own before he moves into the app. He breathes, blinks, follows the pointer, glances at whatever you click, and says a few words. Click anywhere on this page and watch him look.

State

What the app will set from the sync engine: the connection is gone, a sync failed, all is well.

Words

The greeting plays once when the window opens, by the hour. Encouragements come now and then on their own (every two minutes on this page) and only when nothing else is going on.

Reactions

Twenty things that happen while the bridge runs, each with a pose, a prop and a line. Hover a button for the event behind it. Clicking Herbert himself is a poke.

His small life

Left alone and idle he does these on his own, now and then (every 25 seconds on this page; the API default is four minutes): coffee breaks, snacks, stretches, a sneeze, daydreams, humming, reading a letter, a bit of trivia, ear twitches and tail flicks. Once a day: the weekday in the first greeting, a lunch nudge, an evening wind-down, a weekend remark, a wish on a shooting star at night, his birthday. He gets drowsy after long inactivity and welcomes you back after a long absence.

Touch him

Click his nose, ears, horn, bag, tail or belly; press and hold to pet him; double-click for a dance; swirl the pointer over him to make him dizzy; stare (hover) for five seconds; type the word herbert anywhere. Click the speech bubble to dismiss it, hover it to keep it. He is keyboard-focusable: Tab to him, Enter pokes. Put him to sleep and poke him: the internet may be gone, but he still tells you that you’re amazing.

Settings the app will have

Chattiness, quiet hours, your name in his lines, a season to try on, the bag's pending count, the busy spinner, compact size, and the off switch (he leaves; a small horn brings him back).

Appearance

As in the app: the panel stays dark purple, the page and Herbert's speech bubble follow the theme.

What the app will call

Also available in the browser console as herbert — try herbert.say('hello') or herbert.setState('asleep') or herbert.react('delivered').
